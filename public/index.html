Our story

Built on experience from more than one market.

G92 Logistics is led by people who've done this before, running routes and managing DSP operations in California and Florida before bringing that experience to Washington. We've seen what works and what doesn't, and we're building G92 around the lessons, not starting from scratch.

Community

Every package, someone's neighborhood.

Right now, we deliver in downtown Tacoma, North Tacoma, Federal Way, Fife, and Milton. Like every DSP, our coverage shifts as Amazon reassigns routes. Where we're routed has changed before and may change again, but it's always close to our home station. Wherever we're delivering, it's a real neighborhood to us, often close to where our own drivers live too.
